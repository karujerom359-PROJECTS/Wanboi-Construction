About Wanboi Construction

Building Homes. Building Futures.

Wanboi Construction is a locally owned Papua New Guinean building and construction company based in Lae, Morobe Province. We specialise in residential house designs, architectural plans, and full construction services that are practical, durable, and suited to PNG conditions.

Our focus is simple: help ordinary PNG families achieve the dream of owning a quality home without unnecessary cost or complexity. Whether you need complete house plans and material lists to build yourself, or a trusted team to construct the entire project, we are here to support you.

We are comfortable working on projects of any scale and can deliver services anywhere in Papua New Guinea — from urban centres to rural communities.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

🎯

Our Mission

To provide practical, affordable, and high-quality residential building solutions that empower Papua New Guinean families to own safe, comfortable, and well-designed homes. We deliver clear plans, transparent guidance, and reliable construction support at every step.

🌟

Our Vision

To become one of Papua New Guinea’s most trusted names in affordable residential construction — known for quality workmanship, honest pricing, and a genuine commitment to helping more families build a better tomorrow.

What Guides Us

Our Core Values

These principles shape how we design, build, and serve every client.

🏠

Quality First

We design and build homes that stand the test of PNG’s climate and daily family life. Good materials and sound construction are non-negotiable.

💬

Transparency

Clear plans, honest cost guidance, and open communication. You always know what you are getting and what it is likely to cost.

🤝

Partnership

We treat every project as a partnership. We listen to your needs, respect your budget, and work with you — not just for you.

🌏

Local Understanding

As a PNG company based in Lae, we understand local materials, building practices, weather challenges, and community realities.

💡

Practical Design

Our house designs prioritise functionality, ventilation, natural light, and spaces that suit real PNG family living.

🌱

Affordability

We believe quality homes should be within reach. Our designs and advice are structured to help clients build smart within realistic budgets.
