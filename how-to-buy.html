Understanding Construction Costs

The price of the plan package is separate from the final cost of building the house. Your total construction cost will vary based on several important factors:

  • Your location and site conditions
  • Current material prices in your area
  • Transport and delivery costs
  • Labour rates in your region
  • Choice of finishes and fittings
  • Whether you build yourself or hire contractors

Our designs are intentionally planned so that quality homes can be achieved under K200,000 in many parts of Papua New Guinea when carefully managed.
