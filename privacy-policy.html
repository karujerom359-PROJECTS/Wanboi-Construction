Privacy Policy

How Wanboi Construction Ltd collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Wanboi Construction Ltd (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is committed to protecting the privacy of individuals who visit our website or contact us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you interact with us:

  • Name and contact details (phone number, email address, postal address)
  • Location or province
  • Details about your enquiry or project (design interest, budget range, messages)
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide through forms, email, phone or WhatsApp

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data unless you choose to provide it.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and provide the services you request
  • Process plan purchases and related communications
  • Discuss construction projects and provide quotations
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with legal obligations if required

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We may share information only:

  • With service providers who assist us in operating our business (under appropriate confidentiality arrangements)
  • When required by law or to protect our legal rights
  • With your explicit consent

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information we hold from misuse, loss, unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or update it. You may also request that we delete your information, subject to any legal retention requirements. To make a request, please contact us using the details below.

7. Cookies and Website Data

Our website may use basic cookies or similar technologies for essential functionality and analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.

9. Contact Us
