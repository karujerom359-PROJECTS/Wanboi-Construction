Building Guide & Resources

Practical advice to help you plan, budget and build your home successfully in Papua New Guinea.

Start Here

Key Things to Consider Before You Build

Good preparation saves money, time and stress. These are the areas we recommend every client think about early.

💰

Budget Realistically

Your total cost will include more than just materials and labour.

  • Materials (including wastage)
  • Transport to site
  • Labour rates in your area
  • Site preparation and foundations
  • Finishes and fittings
  • Contingency for unexpected costs
📍

Know Your Site

The land itself has a big influence on design and cost.

  • Is the land flat, sloping or low-lying?
  • Access for delivery of materials
  • Soil type and drainage
  • Distance from town / suppliers
  • Any local building requirements
🏠

Choose the Right Design

Match the house to your family size, lifestyle and budget.

  • Number of bedrooms needed now and later
  • Open living vs separate rooms
  • Verandah and outdoor space
  • Elevated vs low-set
  • Future expansion possibilities
🛠️

Decide How You Will Build

There is more than one way to get the house built.

  • Buy plans and manage the build yourself
  • Hire local trades with the plans
  • Engage a full construction company
  • Mix of self-management and hired help
Smart Building

Practical Tips for PNG Home Builders

Lessons that help clients avoid common problems and stay on track.

1

Get a Clear Material List

A proper material list helps you compare supplier prices and avoid over-ordering or running short mid-build.

2

Allow for Transport

In many parts of PNG, getting materials to site can cost as much as the materials themselves. Factor this in early.

3

Prioritise Structure First

Focus budget on a strong frame, good roof and solid foundations. Finishes can be upgraded later if needed.

4

Plan for Weather

Rain can delay work significantly. Schedule critical stages (foundations, roofing) with the local weather pattern in mind.

5

Keep Good Records

Track payments, material deliveries and agreements. Clear records reduce disputes and help you stay organised.

6

Ask Questions Early

Talk to us (or other experienced builders) before you commit money. Early advice is usually the cheapest advice.

Why Many Clients Choose Elevated Designs

Elevated houses on galvanized steel stump posts are popular across Papua New Guinea for good reasons.

They offer practical benefits that suit both urban and rural sites and can help protect your investment over the long term.

  • ✓ Better airflow and cooler living spaces
  • ✓ Reduced risk from ground moisture and flooding
  • ✓ Useful underfloor storage or workspace
  • ✓ Durable galvanized steel stump solution
  • ✓ Easier inspection and maintenance of the structure
  • ✓ Strong visual presence and street appeal
